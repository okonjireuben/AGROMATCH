## 🌟 Overview
REUBEN FOOTBALL STORE is an authentic football retail e-commerce platform dedicated to genuine club & international jerseys, elite boots, match balls, training equipment, and gear.

### Features
- **Authentic Club & National Kits**: 28 premier clubs and national teams across Premier League, La Liga, Ligue 1, Bundesliga, Serie A, and International squads.
- **Elite Football Boots**: High-precision boots from Nike, Adidas, and Puma (Mercurial, Predator 24, Future 7, F50, Tiempo, Phantom Luna).
- **Match Balls**: Official FIFA World Cup balls (including the Telstar 18), UEFA Champions League Pro, and Nike Flight.
- **Interactive Kit Customizer**: Custom name, number, sleeve badges, font styles, and size selection with live preview.
- **Smart Cart & Checkout**: Real-time totals, voucher codes, multiple payment methods, and instant order confirmations.
- **100% Accurate Product Assets**: 41 high-resolution vector assets stored locally in `public/products/`.
